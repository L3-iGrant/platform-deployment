# DPoD key stores

Organisation admins can add their own Thales Luna Cloud HSM (DPoD) partition as a key store. The platform then creates and uses that organisation's signing keys in its own partition, and the keys cannot leave it. Many organisations can share a group of HSM signers, or an organisation can have a group of its own.

We set up this capability with you, based on your requirements. Contact support@igrant.io.
